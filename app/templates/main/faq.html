Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about booking, verification, payments, and policy rules

Before any service provider can appear in customer search results or accept bookings, they must submit a valid Government ID and proof of address. FixMate administrators manually review each application and verify documents through a secure, encrypted viewer before granting APPROVED status.

Providers are allotted a strict 5-minute response window for each new booking request. If a technician does not respond within 5 minutes, our automated scheduling engine offers the booking to the next best-matched available provider in your locality, or safely notifies you if no alternative is free.

Payment is made through our simulated checkout (UPI, Card, Net Banking, or Wallet) after the job is completed. Every bill calculates 18% GST (CGST 9% + SGST 9%) and a downloadable official tax invoice is generated. Provider payouts deduct a 10% platform commission and are held in escrow until confirmed or for 24 hours.

Customers can submit a review and rating (1 to 5 stars) after any completed job. Furthermore, you may lodge a formal complaint ticket with optional photo evidence within 7 days of service completion. FixMate administrators investigate all complaints and can issue full or partial refunds.
