Terms of Service

Last updated: August 2026 | Document Reference: SRS Section 6.0

1. Agreement to Terms

By registering for or using FixMate ("Platform"), you agree to be bound by these Terms of Service. FixMate connects homeowners ("Customers") with independent background-verified workers ("Service Providers").

2. Service Provider Verification (KYC)

Every Service Provider must undergo identity and address verification before accepting bookings. Providers agree to upload authentic government-issued identity cards and proofs of address. Falsification of documents constitutes immediate termination and account suspension.

3. Booking and 5-Minute Acceptance Window

When a Customer requests a booking, the designated Provider has five (5) minutes to accept or decline the request. If the Provider does not respond within this window, the platform will automatically attempt to offer the job to the next best-matched available provider, or mark it as unassigned.

4. Cancellation and Late Fees

Customers may cancel any booking free of charge while in PENDING status. For confirmed and ACCEPTED bookings, cancellations initiated within fifteen (15) minutes of the scheduled appointment or on the same calendar date will incur a cancellation fee (default 10% of quoted price) to compensate the provider for reserved travel and preparation time.

5. Invoicing and Payments

All service bills include an 18% Goods & Services Tax (GST). A 10% platform commission is retained by FixMate. Payments are held in escrow until the customer confirms completion or after a 24-hour dispute-free window has passed.

6. Dispute and Complaints Policy

Customers may lodge formal complaints or dispute charges within seven (7) calendar days of service completion. Administrators review submitted evidence and reserve the right to issue partial or full refunds.
